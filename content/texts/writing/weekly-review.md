# Original version
I have many tasks last week and I don't finish all of them. Next week I want planning better.

# Corrected version
I had many tasks last week, and I did not finish all of them. Next week, I want to plan better.

## Notes
Используем Past Simple для завершённой недели: `had`, `did not finish`.
